# MyCompany Sales Performance & Customer Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance using MySQL and a simulated Nigerian beverage distribution dataset.

The analysis focuses on customer purchasing behavior, product performance, revenue generation, product categories, store performance, and sales trends over time.

The goal is to use SQL to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that can support better sales and customer-related decisions.

> **Note:** The dataset used in this project is simulated and does not contain confidential or proprietary company data.

## Business Problem

A beverage distribution business generates large amounts of sales transaction data across different customers, products, stores, and locations.

Without proper analysis, it can be difficult to determine:

* Which customers generate the most revenue
* Which products perform best
* Which product categories contribute the most revenue
* Which stores perform best
* How sales change from month to month
* Which customers should receive greater attention
* Where opportunities for improving sales performance exist

This project uses SQL to analyze these areas and convert transactional data into actionable business insights.

## Dataset

The dataset is a simulated sales database designed to represent a Nigerian beverage distribution business.

It contains information about:

* Products and their categories
* Customers and customer types
* Stores and their locations
* Staff responsible for sales transactions
* Customer orders
* Products included in each order
* Product prices, quantities, and discounts

### Dataset Characteristics

* **Database:**` mycompanysales_db`
* **Database System:** MySQL
* **Data Type:** Simulated transactional sales data
* **Period Covered:** January 2026 – May 2026
* **Products:** 10
* **Customers:** 10
* **Stores:** 4
* **Staff:** 8
* **Orders:** 25
* **Order Items:** 50

Revenue is calculated using:

```text
Revenue = Quantity × Product Price × (1 − Discount)
```

The dataset was created specifically for analytical practice and portfolio development. It does not represent actual confidential company records.

## Database Structure

The database consists of **7 related tables** designed to represent a simplified sales transaction system.

| Table         | Description                                                                            |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `categories`  | Stores product category information                                                    |
| `products`    | Contains product details, categories, and prices                                       |
| `stores`      | Contains store locations and geographic information                                    |
| `staffs`      | Contains staff information and their assigned stores                                   |
| `customers`   | Contains customer information and customer types                                       |
| `orders`      | Records customer orders, sales staff, stores, and order dates                          |
| `order_items` | Contains the individual products, quantities, and discounts associated with each order |

### Table Relationships

The main relationships in the database are:

```text
categories
     │
     └── products
             │
             └── order_items
                     │
                     └── orders
                          │
              ┌───────────┼───────────┐
              │           │           │
          customers     staffs      stores
```

### Key Relationships

* `categories.category_id` → `products.category_id`
* `products.product_id` → `order_items.product_id`
* `orders.order_id` → `order_items.order_id`
* `customers.customer_id` → `orders.customer_id`
* `staffs.staff_id` → `orders.staff_id`
* `stores.store_id` → `orders.store_id`
* `staffs.store_id` → `stores.store_id`

This relational structure allows sales transactions to be analyzed across customers, products, categories, staff, stores, and time.

## Key Findings

Analysis of the sales data produced the following key findings:

### 1. Overall Revenue

The business generated a total revenue of approximately **₦10.08 million** during the period analyzed.

### 2. Top-Performing Customers

The three highest-revenue customers were:

| Rank | Customer              |    Revenue |
| ---- | --------------------- | ---------: |
| 1    | Metro Supermarket     | ₦1,442,775 |
| 2    | Westline Distributors | ₦1,434,175 |
| 3    | Northern Drinks Ltd   | ₦1,346,850 |

These customers represent important accounts that could have a significant impact on overall sales performance.

### 3. Top-Performing Products

The highest-revenue products were:

| Rank | Product            |    Revenue |
| ---- | ------------------ | ---------: |
| 1    | Golden Oak Whisky  | ₦1,676,400 |
| 2    | Royal Crown Whisky | ₦1,433,195 |
| 3    | Imperial Brandy    | ₦1,287,300 |

Golden Oak Whisky was the highest-revenue product in the dataset.

### 4. Category Performance

Whisky generated the highest revenue among all product categories, contributing approximately **₦3.11 million**.

The category ranking was:

1. Whisky — ₦3,109,595
2. Gin — ₦2,270,150
3. Brandy — ₦2,187,675
4. Vodka — ₦1,542,125
5. Rum — ₦968,100

### 5. Monthly Revenue Performance

The highest monthly revenue was recorded in **March 2026**, with approximately **₦2.87 million**.

| Month    |    Revenue |
| -------- | ---------: |
| January  | ₦1,379,785 |
| February | ₦1,819,200 |
| March    | ₦2,873,735 |
| April    | ₦1,445,950 |
| May      | ₦2,558,975 |

March represented the strongest month, while January recorded the lowest revenue.

### 6. Store Performance

The highest-revenue store was the **Lagos Central Store**, generating approximately **₦3.61 million**.

Store performance was:

| Store                     |    Revenue |
| ------------------------- | ---------: |
| Lagos Central Store       | ₦3,613,850 |
| Abuja Central Store       | ₦3,262,310 |
| Ikeja Distribution Store  | ₦1,865,675 |
| Ibadan Distribution Store | ₦1,335,810 |

Overall, Lagos Central Store and Abuja Central Store were the strongest-performing locations.

### 7. Customer Value

Five customers generated at least **₦1 million** in revenue and can therefore be considered high-value customers based on the project's classification criteria.

These customers represent key accounts that may deserve greater retention and relationship-management attention.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following business actions are recommended:

### 1. Prioritize High-Value Customers

The highest-revenue customers should receive increased account-management attention, including stronger relationship management, retention strategies, and targeted commercial offers.

### 2. Focus on High-Performing Products

Products such as **Golden Oak Whisky, Royal Crown Whisky, and Imperial Brandy** generated strong revenue. Management should ensure adequate availability of these products and monitor their demand closely.

### 3. Strengthen Whisky Category Performance

Whisky was the highest-revenue category in the dataset. The business should continue monitoring demand within this category while identifying opportunities to increase the performance of lower-performing categories.

### 4. Investigate Monthly Sales Fluctuations

Revenue varied significantly across the five months analyzed. The business should investigate the factors behind strong months such as March and May and determine whether similar conditions can be replicated.

### 5. Leverage Strong Store Performance

Lagos Central Store and Abuja Central Store recorded the strongest overall revenue. Sales strategies and practices contributing to their performance could be studied and potentially applied to lower-performing locations.

### 6. Use Data for Customer Segmentation

Revenue-based customer classification can help management prioritize customers and allocate sales resources more effectively.

### 7. Continue Monitoring Performance

Regular SQL-based reporting can help management track revenue, customer activity, product performance, and store performance over time, enabling faster identification of opportunities and potential problems.
